Terms of Reference Framework

The official scope of the Independent Committee used as the primary organizing structure.

ToR 1 — Causes & Circumstances of the Fire
Rapid spread, casualties, property damage, fire causes.
ToR 2 — Fire Service Installations & Equipment
Supervision and responsibility for effective operation.
ToR 3 — Building Maintenance & Renovation Works
Safety requirements, materials list, verification systems.
ToR 4 — Roles & Responsibilities
Government officers, authorised professionals, contractors.
ToR 5 — Systemic Issues
Connected interests, conflicts, collusion, bid-rigging.
ToR 6 + 7 — Laws, Penalties & Recommendations
Adequacy of regulation and improvement measures.
