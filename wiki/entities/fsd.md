# Fire Services Department (FSD)

- **Role:** fire service installations, licensing of contractors, inspection and enforcement.
- **Responsibility finding:** named in Ch7 §1187 (fire-safety failures).
- **Witnesses:** Director of Fire Services (T19), Deputy Director (Operations) (T18),
  Assistant Directors (T10, T17), station officers (T11), plus the 1982 code of practice on
  inspection and testing discussed at T10.
- **Policy questions raised in evidence:** the "current policy … no surprise checks" on
  inspections [T10]; the shutdown-notice regime [T9, T10].
- **Policy owner:** FSD sets operational policy; the bureau above it (Security Bureau) gave
  no evidence.
