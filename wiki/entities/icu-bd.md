# Independent Checking Unit (ICU) and Buildings Department (BD)

- **ICU** — unit of the Housing Bureau that carries out building-control functions for HOS
  estates under delegated authority; "reports to the Permanent Secretary of the Housing
  Bureau" [T29/…]; "policy-wise … follow[s] the Buildings Department's practice — to put it
  bluntly, blindly" [T30/…].
- **BD** — the building-control authority; sets the standards the ICU applies.
- **Responsibility finding:** Ch7 §1187 places responsibility for fire-safety failures on
  "the ICU/BD" among others.
- **Institutional responsibility quote:** "The BD bears institutional responsibility both for
  the inadequacy of its own standards and for its inability to ensure the ICU … maintained
  even those standards that the BD did have." [Ch7]
- **Evidence base:** ICU witnesses Ku Siu Ping (WS14, 24 citations), senior building
  maintenance surveyors (T22–T24); BD Assistant Director (T20).
- **Policy owner:** Housing Bureau (policy) / BD (standards) — neither gave evidence.
- **Hearing days:** T20 (27 Apr, 130 policy terms — highest of any session), T22 (6 May, Ku Siu Ping, standard 29), T23–T24.
