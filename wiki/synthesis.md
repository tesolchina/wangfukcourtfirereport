# Synthesis — what the record shows, and what it does not

**Working thesis (status: supported by Group 1, to be tested against Groups 2–7):**

> The inquiry heard from the people who applied the rules, not the people who wrote them —
> and it recommends rewriting the rules while assigning responsibility only to the
> departments that applied them.
