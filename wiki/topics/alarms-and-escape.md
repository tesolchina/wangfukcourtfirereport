# Alarms, water tanks and escape

- **Public concern (Nov 2025):** residents did not hear alarms; stairwells unusable.
- **In the record:** alarms deactivated in seven of eight blocks; water tanks drained;
  85 shutdown-renewal notices filed without site visits; 999 call-handling examined [T12].
- **In the argument:** Ch4 (spread) and Ch5 (emergency response); Ch8 recommendations.
- **Note:** this is the most implementation-heavy topic in the record — and the one where
  department witnesses were most numerous (FSD, 15 timetable entries).
