# Flame-retardant netting: guideline vs mandatory standard

- **Public concern (Nov 2025):** non-flame-retardant nets on bamboo scaffolding; reporting
  noted that flame-retardant nets were **a guideline, not a mandatory standard**.
- **In the record:** the ICU accepted certificates without a basic check of the issuing
  institution; a false certificate was used for replacement nets [T22, WS14].
- **In the argument:** Ch7 discusses "the adequacy of the standards … and the effectiveness
  of testing and verification systems"; Ch8 recommends reform.
- **Gap:** the standard's *author* (policy level) gave no evidence; the failure is examined
  as an implementation failure (verification) rather than as a policy choice.
