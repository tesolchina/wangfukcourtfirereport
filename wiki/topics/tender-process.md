# The HK$330m renovation and its tendering

- **Public concern (Nov 2025):** "sky-high" renovation cost, black-box tendering, ignored
  resident complaints, owners' corporation governance.
- **In the record:** Ch2 (tender process, ~24 pp) and Ch9 (bid-rigging landscape); the
  Committee's counsel notes it is "not required … to make findings on the existence of such
  issues in the case of Wang Fuk Court", while recording "indicia" (unreasonable bid prices,
  connections between bidders, concealed litigation record).
- **Witnesses:** URA staff (T21, the most-cited day), owners' corporation office-bearers
  (T14–T15), Competition Commission (closing submission).
- **Gap:** the procurement *rules* (policy) are discussed by counsel, not tested with the
  officials who set them.
