## Reading
1. **Formulation vocabulary is not scarce.** 66 of 118 witness statements and 28 of 30
   transcripts are policy-heavy even at the summary level — the record is full of talk about
   standards, guidelines, legislation and penalties.
2. **The heaviest policy documents are argument documents**: the Committee's closing address
   (f=13), the Government's closing address (f=14), the Usmani/Jiang recommendations (f=15),
   the Competition Commission's opening (f=7).
3. **Contractors' legal replies are policy-heavy too** (f=5–8) — they argue about the rules
   they were governed by.
4. So the "policy deficit" is **not about how often policy is mentioned** — it is about
   **who is asked**: the formulation vocabulary flows through implementers, contractors,
   counsel and experts; no policy-branch witness appears.

## Caveat
Scores are computed on **summaries**, not full text (the summaries were generated from the
full documents; see `sources/group-1...` and Group 2 for full-text checks). Treat the table
as a screening instrument, not a measurement of argument weight.
