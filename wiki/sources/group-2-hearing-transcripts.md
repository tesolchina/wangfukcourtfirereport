## Reading
1. **Policy questions were asked — of department officers.** The heaviest policy-formulation
   vocabulary appears on department days: BD/ICU (T20, 130), FSD (T10, 108; T17, 107),
   Labour (T16, 95), ICU (T22, 71), HAD (T24, 98). The questions ("why was the requirement
   set this way?", "who approved?", "what does the guideline require?") were put to the
   people who applied the rules.
2. **Residents' days are the least policy-oriented** (T4 = 1 policy term in 138k chars;
   T13 = 5) — the hearings treat residents as witnesses to events, not to rules.
3. **Experts carry the standards vocabulary** (T25: standard 59) — but they describe the
   behaviour of materials and systems, not the decisions that set standards.
4. **No session is a bureau session.** The policy vocabulary is distributed across
   implementing bodies; there is no day whose witness set is a policy branch.

## Consequence for the thesis
The claim "policy was never discussed" is **false**. The accurate claim is:
**policy was discussed with the people who implemented it, on days organised around
implementing bodies** — the formulation questions were asked of the wrong level.
